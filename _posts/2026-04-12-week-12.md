# Open Source and the Industry

I found it very smart that Red Hat made money on the fact that the Open Source community is extremely decentralized by providing an organized way to support the software. It is a cool idea that they combined both the software created by talented Open Source developers with the reliability of a proprietary object.

It is good to know that tech companies are beginning to tangibly support open source projects. It is especially interesting, since no single one of those companies runs charities, and I am sure they wouldn't part with their money unless those Open Source projects are truly integral to their operations. Although still, I am not over, for example, Discord allocation only $200 a year towards a project!!(??) Tech companies could definitely do more for Open Source, and I can imagine those communities would absolutely flourish with a little extra monetary incentive and some recognition. And a flourishing community, an Open Source community == well-maintained and well-written piece of software that any of those companies can use.
