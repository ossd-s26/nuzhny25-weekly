## Cathedral vs Bazaar

I find the idea of bazaar fascinating! It is so cool to me that decentralized systems and communities can compete and, in some cases, outperform proprietary software. Of course, Open Source has its own issues, like the illusion that, in reality, not that many people are actually looking at the code and ensuring its quality, or the vulnerability to malicious actors submitting malicious code. However, in the end, open source always wins in my opinion because it isn't done for profit and isn't usually tied to any financial institution. To submit a piece of malicious code, you must go through the maintainers. Even if I succeed in that, it will get noticed by someone sooner or later and fixed. While in proprietary software there is a monetary incentive to sweep breaches under the rug, and it is much harder to discover them since fewer people are looking at the code. I believe AI tools won't significantly change the landscape of those two models; however, they will allow software engineers and maintainers to be more productive.
