## Oppia Work 

Over the past week, my team and I have been focused on trying to get some prs in before our presentation on May 4th. All of us are on track to have at least one pr sent by then. However, the more I work on Oppia, the more I realize how much I hate it. Currently, I should be done with the code for my pr; however, it turns out running a unit test to check if it works is not that easy. I spent 4 hours yesterday trying to run the tests, but it's just not happening. I tried running all of the tests, specific module tests, specific class of tests, and even the specific test that I needed, but all I got was a timeout error. If I were being honest, if I weren't working on Oppia for a grade, I would've quit a long time ago (probably when it took me several days to get the developer environment running). But on the other hand, maybe I am being too optimistic, but after this experience, I bet that the rest of the Open Source should feel like a breath of fresh air, so I am very excited for the summer, so I can dedicate myself to contributing to Open Source projects!
